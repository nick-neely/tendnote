What does a stranger go through, from choosing the offer to First Value?

This page lets you walk a new United States customer through the proposed hosted Tendnote journey: the landing page, the offer, creating an account, paying through Stripe, being admitted, the first-run path, and the four steps that make up First Value. It also covers the awkward cases: a declined card, a closed tab, a slow payment confirmation, an invited Household member, an existing private-beta account, a life-admin first capture, and what happens to billing later. The left column is the model's state; the phone is what the customer would see. The screen copy is a proposal for reaction, not final wording. Nothing here talks to Stripe or a database.
