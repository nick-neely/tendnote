What does the Household Guest experience look and feel like?

Tilly accepted a Household Invitation to a household somebody else pays for. She is a Household Guest: an unpaid hosted account with read-only sight of what the household shares, no Eve, no capture, no reminders, no exports, and no private records of any member. This page proposes three structurally different answers to what she actually opens. Use the pill at the bottom to switch between them, and the scenario panel on the left to drive whichever one is showing through entry, browsing, a refusal, subscribing, the household losing its last paying Owner, its return, and removal. Nothing here talks to a database or to Stripe; the copy is a proposal for reaction.

Questions this prototype asks the owner
  1. Which surface? A single digest page, a domain-tabbed read-only library, or a preview of the real shell.
  2. How much should it resemble the product? Familiarity sells the subscription; resemblance also invites the guest to expect authority they do not have.
  3. Should paid-only features be visible at all? Variant C lists Eve, capture, Saved Items and reminders as locked entries. A and B omit them entirely. Visible means honest and persuasive; absent means calm and never teasing.
  4. Where does the subscribe path live, and how insistent is it? A: one card at the bottom of the scroll. B: one line in the persistent read-only band. C: a standing sidebar pitch plus every locked entry.
  5. What does the sponsor-lapsed page say? The draft states the fact without blame, gives no billing detail about anyone else, and offers the guest only their own subscribe path.
  6. Does the guest see who the Owners are in that state? The draft says no: no names, no "ask Adaeze to fix it". Confirm that, or soften it to "the household's Owners have been told".
  7. Does the guest keep any presence at all after removal, or does the household simply vanish from their account?
