# Tendnote contribution agreement packet

**Status: DRAFT — PENDING COUNSEL APPROVAL**

This directory contains proposed final-form contribution agreements for
Tendnote and Neely Solutions LLC. Every document here is a draft. None is
effective, and no one should accept one, until counsel has approved the exact
text and the owner updates the status in this repository. Publication of a
draft is not legal advice, a rights grant, or evidence that CLA Assistant is
configured.

## Choose the route

Use the narrowest route that matches who owns or controls the work:

1. **The contributor owns the contribution:** use the [Individual Contributor
   License Agreement (ICLA)](individual-contributor-license-agreement.md).
2. **An employer owns a single contribution or a narrow named-contributor
   scope:** use the [Employer Contribution
   Authorization](employer-contribution-authorization.md), signed by an
   authorized employer representative, together with the individual route
   where required.
3. **An entity will make recurring contributions through named people:** use
   the reusable [Corporate Contributor License
   Agreement](corporate-contributor-license-agreement.md) and keep its
   authorized-contributor schedule current. An individual agreement may still
   be needed for work the entity does not own.

The employer and corporate routes are rights-authority records, not an
undocumented bypass of the individual contribution gate. The selected
agreement must be accepted or signed through the approved process before the
associated contribution is eligible for merge. The project has not claimed
that this process is live; operational configuration and external-contributor
proof belong to [issue #473](https://github.com/nick-neely/tendnote/issues/473).

## Records and status

Signed agreements, employer authorizations, schedules, contact details, and
electronic acceptance records are private. A pull request may show only the
minimum public CLA status needed for a merge check; CLA status may be public,
but it does not expose the agreement or personal details.

The owner must update the status of each exact version after counsel approval.
Until that happens, all three forms remain drafts and are not available for
acceptance. No agent or contributor may self-certify counsel approval, change
this status, or execute an agreement on behalf of another person or entity.

## Drafting sources

The proposed language is original to Tendnote and was informed by the [Apache
ICLA v2.2](https://www.apache.org/licenses/icla.pdf), the [Apache Corporate
Contributor License Agreement](https://www.apache.org/licenses/cla-corporate.pdf),
the [Linux Foundation generative-AI
guidance](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/legal/generative-ai), and Apache's
[generative tooling guidance](https://www.apache.org/legal/generative-tooling.html).
Those sources are background only and are not incorporated into any draft.
They do not replace counsel review of the exact Tendnote text.
