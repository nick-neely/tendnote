To the extent the employer can grant it, the employer also grants a perpetual,

## 3. Employer representations

The Authorizing Employer represents that:

1. the signer is an authorized employer representative with authority to make
   this authorization and grant the licenses above;
2. the authorization is consistent with the employer's contracts, policies,
   confidentiality obligations, and third-party commitments;
3. the named contributor is authorized to submit the stated scope; and
4. the employer will identify any limitation, excluded material, required
   attribution, third-party right, patent, trademark, or other restriction
   that applies to the scope.

The named contributor remains responsible for the contributor's own
representations, disclosures, and acceptance of the individual agreement. This
form does not excuse disclosure of third-party material or permit confidential
material to be placed in a public issue or pull request.

## 4. Third-party material and AI-assisted Contributions

The employer and named contributor must disclose known third-party material in
the authorized scope, including its source, rightsholder, attribution, and
applicable license or restriction, and must obtain any permission needed to
modify and submit it.

AI-assisted Contributions are permitted. The named contributor remains the
human responsible for reviewing and understanding the submitted result. The
employer and contributor must ensure that the applicable AI-tool terms allow
submission under the Project's license and policies. If either knows that AI
output includes pre-existing third-party material, they must confirm permission
and provide the Section 3/4 disclosure before submission. Optional public
disclosure may identify only provider/model and role; a `Generated-by:` trailer
is optional. The Project does not request prompts, raw outputs, account
information, or usage data.

## 5. No support; contribution provided as-is

The employer and named contributor have no obligation to provide support,
maintenance, updates, or services for the authorized contribution. To the
fullest extent permitted by law, it is provided **AS IS**, without express or
implied warranties or conditions, including title, non-infringement,
merchantability, or fitness for a particular purpose. This does not exclude an
obligation that applicable law does not permit the parties to exclude.

## 6. Notice, records, and no bypass

The employer must promptly notify Neely Solutions LLC if its authority, named
contributor, scope, ownership, third-party permission, or other representation
changes. A replacement or revocation notice applies prospectively from its
recorded effective date and does not withdraw rights already granted for a
submitted contribution.

The signed authorization and contact details are private. A pull request may
show only the minimum status needed to establish that the required route is on
file. This form is not an undocumented bypass of the individual contribution
gate, and it does not claim that CLA Assistant or default-branch enforcement is
currently live.
