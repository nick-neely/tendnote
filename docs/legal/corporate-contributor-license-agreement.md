# Tendnote Corporate Contributor License Agreement

**Version 1.0 — DRAFT — PENDING COUNSEL APPROVAL**

**Important status notice:** This reusable entity agreement is a proposed
final-form draft for counsel review. It is not effective until the owner
updates the status after counsel approval. It must not be signed, accepted, or
described as approved while it is marked **DRAFT — PENDING COUNSEL APPROVAL**.
It is not legal advice.

This agreement is intended for an entity that will make recurring contributions
to Tendnote through an authorized contributor schedule. The exact legal names,
addresses, notices, and any governing-law terms must be confirmed before the
owner changes this document's status.

## 2. Entity ownership and license grant

The Entity represents that it owns or controls, or is authorized by the owner
to grant, the rights in Contributions submitted within the schedule. The Entity
retains all right, title, and interest not expressly granted by this agreement;
this is a license, not an assignment of the Entity's copyright.

For each covered Contribution, the Entity grants every Recipient a perpetual,
worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright license to
reproduce, modify, prepare derivative works from, publicly display, publicly
perform, distribute, communicate, and otherwise make the Contribution and
derivative works available. The grant includes the right to sublicense and
relicense in whole or in part for current or future open-source, commercial,
proprietary, hosted, service, or larger-work distributions.

To the extent the Entity can grant it, the Entity grants every Recipient a
perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free patent license to make, have
made, use, offer to sell, sell, import, and otherwise transfer the Project as
it contains each covered Contribution. The patent grant is limited to patent
claims the Entity may license that are necessarily infringed by the
Contribution alone or by the Contribution combined with the Project to which
it was submitted. It excludes unrelated combinations and claims outside the
Entity's authority.

If a Recipient initiates patent litigation against the Entity or another
person or entity alleging that a covered Contribution or the relevant Project
infringes a patent, that litigating Recipient's patent license for the affected
Contribution and Project terminates when the litigation is filed. The
termination is limited to that patent license and does not terminate the
copyright license or an unaffected Contribution or Project.

## 4. Update duties

The Entity must promptly notify Neely Solutions LLC of additions, removals,
scope changes, changes to the point of contact, and any change that could make
an authority, ownership, third-party, or tool-term representation inaccurate.
Each schedule update must identify the agreement version, affected GitHub
account, effective date, scope change, and authorized Entity representative.
Neely Solutions LLC may require a fresh acceptance or signed update before a
new contributor or scope is eligible for merge.

The Entity remains responsible for Contributions submitted by a listed person
before the effective removal or scope change. Schedule updates apply
prospectively from their recorded effective date and do not withdraw rights
already granted for a submitted Contribution.

## 5. Authority and third-party material

The Entity represents that its signatory has authority to enter this agreement
and that the agreement is consistent with its contracts, policies,
confidentiality duties, and third-party commitments. The Entity and each
authorized contributor must identify known material that is not the Entity's or
contributor's original work, including source, rightsholder, attribution, and
applicable license, patent, trademark, permission, or restriction. They must
obtain permission needed to modify and submit it. The Entity grants no rights
beyond those it actually controls.

## 6. AI-assisted Contributions

AI-assisted Contributions are permitted. The human authorized contributor
remains responsible for reviewing, understanding, testing, and standing behind
the submitted result. The Entity and contributor must ensure that each
generative AI tool's terms permit submission under the Project's license and
policies and do not impose a conflicting restriction.

If the Entity or contributor knows that AI output included in a Contribution
contains pre-existing third-party material, they must confirm permission to
use, modify, and submit it and must provide source, attribution, and applicable
license or other restriction before submission. This agreement does not require
a representation about unknowable training-data provenance. Optional public
disclosure may identify only provider/model and role; a `Generated-by:` trailer
is optional. The Project does not request prompts, raw outputs, account
information, or usage data.

## 7. No support; contribution provided as-is

The Entity and its authorized contributors have no obligation to provide
support, maintenance, updates, or services for a Contribution. To the fullest
extent permitted by law, each Contribution is provided **AS IS**, without
express or implied warranties or conditions, including title,
non-infringement, merchantability, or fitness for a particular purpose. This
does not exclude an obligation that applicable law does not permit the parties
to exclude.

## 8. Acceptance, records, and no obligation to accept

Neely Solutions LLC may review, reject, omit, modify, remove, or stop
distributing any Contribution. Nothing obligates a Recipient to accept, merge,
publish, maintain, or distribute a Contribution, gives the Entity or an
authorized contributor commit or merge rights, or creates a partnership,
agency, employment, or fiduciary relationship.

The signed agreement, schedule, updates, contact details, and electronic
records are private. A pull request may show only the minimum public status
needed for a merge check. The owner may require each authorized contributor to
complete the approved individual acceptance flow for work outside Entity
ownership or for any additional project policy.

This agreement becomes effective only when the owner updates the status to an
effective version, an authorized Entity representative signs this exact
version, and the approved process retains the record. Before that sequence,
this document is not effective and grants no rights. A new version requires the
acceptance or signature specified by that version; it does not silently amend a
prior accepted version.

This agreement, its schedule, and its retained updates are the entire agreement
between the Entity and Neely Solutions LLC concerning Contributions and
supersede prior discussions or understandings on that subject. Any amendment
or new version must be recorded in writing or electronically and accepted or
signed as required by the new version. If a provision is invalid or
unenforceable, it will be limited or severed to the minimum extent necessary
and the remaining provisions will continue in effect. A waiver of one
provision is not a waiver of another or of a later breach. This agreement may
be signed in electronic counterparts, each of which is an original, and the
retained electronic record is evidence of the accepted version.
