# Tendnote Individual Contributor License Agreement (ICLA)

**Version 1.0 — DRAFT — PENDING COUNSEL APPROVAL**

**Important status notice:** This is a proposed final-form draft for counsel
review. It is not effective until the owner updates the status after counsel
approval. Publication of this file does not invite acceptance and does not
grant Neely Solutions LLC or Tendnote any rights. It is not legal advice.

This proposed agreement is between the person accepting it (the
**Contributor**) and **Neely Solutions LLC**, for contributions to the Tendnote
project (the **Project**). The exact legal names, notices, and any governing-law
terms must be confirmed before the owner changes this document's status.

## 1. Electronic acceptance and records

After this exact version is approved and the owner enables the process, the
Contributor may accept through the Project's configured CLA Assistant flow. The
Contributor must review this agreement and affirmatively accept version 1.0;
opening a pull request, committing code, or typing a name in a pull request is
not acceptance by itself. The acceptance record may identify the Contributor's
legal name, GitHub account and account identifier, contact email, accepted
version, and acceptance timestamp.

Neely Solutions LLC will keep the signed or click-through record private. A
pull request or check may expose only whether the required version is on file.
Electronic records and an electronic acceptance have the same intended effect
as a signed counterpart once this agreement is approved and made effective.

## 2. Definitions

- **Contribution** means an original work of authorship, or an original
  modification or addition to an existing work, that the Contributor
  intentionally submits to the Project for inclusion in, or documentation of,
  the Project. A submission can be made through a pull request, commit, issue,
  review, or another Project channel. Material clearly marked in writing as
  “Not a Contribution” is excluded unless the Contributor later intentionally
  submits it as a Contribution.
- **Submit** means intentionally sending a Contribution to Neely Solutions LLC,
  the Project, or a person acting for either for the purpose described above.
- **Project** means Tendnote and its source, documentation, tests, tools,
  configuration, and successor or related distributions controlled by Neely
  Solutions LLC.
- **Recipient** means Neely Solutions LLC, the Project, and each person or
  entity receiving a Project distribution or a permitted sublicense or
  relicense.
- **Employer** means a current or former employer, client, school, or other
  person or entity that may own or control rights in the Contribution.

## 3. Ownership is retained

Contributor retains ownership: The Contributor retains all right, title, and
interest in the Contributor's
Contribution, including copyright, except for the licenses expressly granted
below. This agreement is not an assignment of copyright and does not prevent
the Contributor from using, licensing, or assigning the Contribution for other
purposes. The Contributor must not use this individual route for material the
Contributor is not legally entitled to license; the employer routes in the
[agreement packet](README.md) address that case.

## 4. Copyright license

Subject to this agreement, the Contributor grants each Recipient a perpetual,
worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright license to
reproduce, modify, prepare derivative works from, publicly display, publicly
perform, distribute, communicate, and otherwise make the Contribution and
derivative works available. The license includes the right to sublicense and
relicense those rights to other recipients, in whole or in part, including for
open-source and commercial distribution under the Project's current or future
open-source, commercial, proprietary, hosted, service, or larger-work license.
No payment, attribution format, or continued use is a condition of this
license, except for obligations imposed by the license selected for a
particular distribution.

## 5. Narrow patent license and termination

To the extent the Contributor can grant it, the Contributor grants each
Recipient a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free patent license
to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import, and otherwise transfer
the Project as it contains the Contribution. This license covers only patent
claims that the Contributor may license and that are necessarily infringed by
the Contribution itself or by the Contribution combined with the Project to
which it was submitted. It does not cover unrelated combinations, later
independent products, or patent claims the Contributor cannot license.

If a Recipient initiates patent litigation against the Contributor or another
person or entity alleging that the Contribution or the Project to which it was
submitted infringes a patent, the patent license granted to that litigating
Recipient for the affected Contribution and Project terminates when the
litigation is filed. The termination is limited to that patent license and
does not terminate the copyright license or any license for an unaffected
Contribution or Project.

## 6. Authority and employer representation

The Contributor represents that:

1. the Contributor is legally entitled to grant the licenses in this
   agreement;
2. the Contribution is the Contributor's original creation except for
   material disclosed under Section 7;
3. submitting the Contribution does not breach a contract, confidentiality
   duty, policy, or other obligation; and
4. if an Employer owns or controls any right in the Contribution, the
   Contributor has written permission or a signed employer authorization or
   corporate agreement that permits this submission and the licenses in this
   agreement.

An individual acceptance does not waive an Employer's rights and does not
convert employer-owned work into Contributor-owned work. The Contributor must
use the employer route before submitting any work for which that route is
needed.

## 7. Third-party material and notices

Before submitting a Contribution, the Contributor must disclose material the
Contributor knows is not the Contributor's original work. The disclosure must
identify the source or rightsholder, the applicable copyright, patent,
trademark, or other license or restriction, required attribution, and any
permission on which the submission relies. The Contributor must identify the
location of that material in the Contribution and mark work submitted on
behalf of another person or entity. Neely Solutions LLC does not receive a
license broader than the rights actually available under the disclosed terms.

If the Contributor later learns that a representation or disclosure was
inaccurate or incomplete, the Contributor must promptly notify the Project and
provide the corrected source, attribution, permission, or restriction.

## 8. AI-assisted Contributions

AI-assisted Contributions are permitted. The human Contributor remains
responsible for reviewing, understanding, testing, and standing behind the
submitted result. The Contributor represents that the AI tool terms applicable
to each generative AI tool used for the Contribution permit the Contributor to
submit the result under the Project's applicable license and policies and do
not impose a conflicting contractual restriction.

If the Contributor knows that AI output included in a Contribution contains
pre-existing third-party material, the Contributor must confirm permission to
use, modify, and submit that material before submitting it, and must disclose
its source, attribution, and applicable license or other restriction under
Section 7. This agreement does not require a representation about unknowable
training-data provenance.

Public disclosure of AI assistance is optional and, if made, may identify only
the provider or model and the tool's role. A `Generated-by:` commit trailer is
also optional. The Project does not request prompts, raw model outputs,
provider account information, or usage data as a condition of contribution or
acceptance.

## 9. No support; contribution provided as-is

The Contributor has no obligation to provide support, maintenance, updates, or
other services for a Contribution. Any voluntary support is provided without
creating an ongoing duty. To the fullest extent permitted by law, the
Contribution is provided **AS IS**, without warranties or conditions of any
kind, express or implied, including title, non-infringement, merchantability,
or fitness for a particular purpose. This section does not exclude a warranty
or obligation that applicable law does not permit the parties to exclude.

## 10. No obligation to accept

Neely Solutions LLC may review, reject, omit, modify, remove, or stop
distributing any Contribution. Nothing in this agreement obligates a Recipient
to accept, merge, publish, maintain, or distribute a Contribution, gives a
Contributor commit or merge rights, or creates a partnership, agency,
employment, or fiduciary relationship.

## 11. Notice, updates, and versions

The Contributor must promptly notify the Project of a change that could make a
representation, employer authorization, third-party disclosure, or AI-tool
statement inaccurate. The Contributor should also update the private contact
and GitHub-account record when it changes.

The owner may publish a new agreement version. A new version does not silently
amend an accepted version; the Project may require a new affirmative
acceptance before accepting contributions under the new version. A status
change cannot be made by an agent or inferred from a merge.

## 12. Effect and acceptance record

This agreement becomes effective only when the owner has published this exact
version as effective, the Contributor has affirmatively accepted it through
the approved process, and the acceptance record is retained. Before that
sequence, this document is not effective and grants no rights.

This agreement and its acceptance record are the entire agreement between the
Contributor and Neely Solutions LLC concerning Contributions and supersede
prior discussions or understandings on that subject. Any amendment or new
version must be recorded in writing or electronically and affirmatively
accepted as required by the new version; it does not silently amend an
accepted version. If a provision is invalid or unenforceable, it will be
limited or severed to the minimum extent necessary and the remaining
provisions will continue in effect. A waiver of one provision is not a waiver
of another or of a later breach. This agreement may be accepted in electronic
counterparts, each of which is an original, and the retained electronic record
is evidence of the accepted version.
