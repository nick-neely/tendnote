# Asset Audit Is Internal First

Phase 6 should record internal audit context for asset writes, reviews, and automated proposals, including who or what created, edited, accepted, dismissed, linked, archived, or proposed Assets, Asset Memories, Asset Evidence, Related Asset Links, and asset-derived Suggested General Actions. Asset Audit supports trust, debugging, provenance, and future trusted-agent modes, but it should not become a user-facing activity feed in Phase 6; user-facing history remains Asset History.
