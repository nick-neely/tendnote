# Household-Aware Assistance Is Caller-Scoped and Member-Delivered

Phase Eight will keep Eve, strategist, Capture, Review, and scheduled work
private by default, adding a deliberate Household context that reads only the
current caller's independently authorized Household coordination records and
uses the owning domain's existing commands. An optional Household Check-in may
make up to three timely, authorized records useful inside that member's in-app
briefing, but it is not a shared digest, household agent, activity feed, or
cross-member delivery mechanism. This chooses caller-scoped usefulness over a
collective assistant: it protects member-owned data, surprise exclusions, and
per-member notification authority while retaining a clear way to coordinate
across the records all involved members deliberately share.

Every candidate is filtered and capped deterministically before generation, is
grounded in canonical records with scope/provenance/freshness treatment, and is
re-authorized at generation and delivery. Generated prose is ephemeral;
inference remains Review-gated; explicit actions reuse domain-owned authority;
and Phase Eight adds no Household email, shared channel, or push delivery. The
full UX, source, lifecycle, and test contract lives in
[`docs/phase-8/household-aware-eve-and-proactive-behavior.md`](../phase-8/household-aware-eve-and-proactive-behavior.md).
