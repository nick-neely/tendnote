# Asset Snapshots Are Rebuildable Caches

Phase 6 should generate Asset Snapshots for fast Asset profile loading and Eve grounding, but they must remain rebuildable caches rather than source of truth. Asset snapshots should cite the Assets, Asset Memories, Asset Evidence, related General Actions, Related Asset Links, Asset Person Links, and Asset History they summarize, and the product should degrade gracefully when a snapshot is stale or missing.
