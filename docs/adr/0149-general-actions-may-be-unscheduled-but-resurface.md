# General Actions May Be Unscheduled But Resurface

Phase 5 should allow General Actions to be due-dated, deferred, recurring, or unscheduled because not every Personal OS action has a real due date. Unscheduled actions should still have explicit resurfacing semantics through age, area, review cadence, or a resurface-around date so they do not disappear or flood proactive briefs; a future explicit signal may be considered separately without making priority part of the current model.
