# Observation-To-Memory Review

Phase 1 distinguishes observations from memories: observations are agent-inferred, low-trust, reviewable context, while memories are user-approved or strongly user-confirmed durable facts. Manual requests such as "remember", "save", "note", or "keep track of" may create approved memories directly, but agent-noticed facts should enter a suggested state and require save, edit, or dismiss review before they become durable relationship context.

Sensitive observations require explicit approval before durable storage even when confidence is high. This keeps Tendnote useful without silently building inaccurate or overly sensitive profiles, and gives future Gmail, Calendar, and message integrations a clear suggested-to-approved pipeline.
