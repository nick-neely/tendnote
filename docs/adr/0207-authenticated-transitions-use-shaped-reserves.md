# Authenticated Transitions Use Shaped Reserves

After admission, Tendnote uses a destination-specific **Shaped Reserve** as the default 0–100 ms navigation state: stable desktop or mobile navigation, the truthful destination heading and static controls, and neutral regions shaped like the major content that will replace them. The reserve preserves final geometry without fabricating owner data, keeps the product's existing flat Field Notebook and Personal Ledger language, and lets each independently useful region resolve in place. Desktop and mobile use the same contract; mobile keeps its orientation and bottom navigation stable while the destination content streams.

A meaningful independent stream may use a **Named Region** instead of anonymous geometry when the name helps the owner understand what remains usable or what failed. Eve, Review families, provider Connections, Reminders, and similar externally authoritative or fail-soft regions qualify; ordinary fast list rows do not need loading prose that makes the transition feel slower.

**Retained Context** is allowed only within the same admitted destination and visibility boundary. During revalidation, mutation reconciliation, or an independent-region refresh, already-resolved truthful siblings remain visible—for example, Connections stays settled while Reminders refreshes, or vice versa—and an affected region may remain inert with local pending feedback when its content is still safe and accurate enough to show. Tendnote does not retain a previous route beneath a different destination: that makes navigation ambiguous, reserves no destination geometry, and risks presenting stale owner context as current.

Failures stay local. Stable navigation, the destination heading, entered text, and successful sibling regions remain available; the failed region explains that records are unchanged and offers a calm retry. Loading, empty, unavailable, and error states remain distinct. The throwaway prototype and capture matrix are preserved on `prototype/instant-shells-292`; none of its rendering code is promoted.
