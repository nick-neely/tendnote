# Phase 5 Defers External Task And Reminder Writes

Phase 5 should keep Actions and Routines Tendnote-owned and defer writes to external task, reminder, calendar, or productivity systems such as Google Tasks, Todoist, Apple Reminders, Notion tasks, or provider-side calendar events. External providers may supply context or scoped delivery surfaces under existing consent rules, but Tendnote should prove its own General Action model before adding outbound synchronization or external action stores.
