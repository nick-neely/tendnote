# Household Relationship Sharing Does Not Create Shared People

Phase Eight keeps every Person and person-centered relationship record owned by
one member. A member may deliberately grant selected Household Members or the
whole Household read access to an individual Memory, Source Record, or
Follow-Up, but visibility never transfers ownership or grants edit, review,
evidence, Person-link, sensitivity, or reminder authority. Records and their
evidence are shared independently, and departure immediately returns the
owner's shared relationship records to private scope.

Household-native coordination records identify an external person only through
a minimal, record-local Person Reference deliberately supplied by an authorized
member. A Person Reference is not a Household Person, shared contact, identity
match, or permission-bearing link into any member's private People graph. This
preserves useful household planning while avoiding an irreversible shared
contact model, cross-member relationship-history merging, surveillance, and CRM
behavior. The full surface, evidence, authority, and lifecycle contract lives in
[`docs/phase-8/household-relationship-records-and-privacy.md`](../phase-8/household-relationship-records-and-privacy.md).
