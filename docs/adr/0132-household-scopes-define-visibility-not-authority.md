# Household Scopes Define Visibility Not Authority

Phase 4 should define `private`, `shared`, and `household` as visibility scopes, not role authority. Private records are visible only to their owning user; shared records are visible only to explicitly selected Household Members on a record-by-record basis; household records are visible to all active Household Members, including future accepted members, and the Household Owner role does not pierce another member's private scope. Removed members lose household/shared record access immediately, except for internal audit retention. Phase 4 should keep the shared-scope UI simple with choices such as only me, selected members, and whole household, while deferring groups, nested permissions, custom roles, expiry windows, and per-field sharing.
