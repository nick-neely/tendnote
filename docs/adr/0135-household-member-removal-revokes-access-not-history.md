# Household Member Removal Revokes Access Not History

Phase 4 member removal should immediately revoke the removed member's access to household and shared records while preserving their own private records and preserving creator provenance on records they created. Household-scoped records remain in the Household Workspace after a member leaves; selected-member shared records remain visible to remaining allowed members when still anchored to the household. Export, deletion, transfer-all-records, and richer offboarding workflows are deferred beyond Phase 4.
