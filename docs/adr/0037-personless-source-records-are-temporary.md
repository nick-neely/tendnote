# Personless Source Records Are Temporary

Phase 1A may create personless source records for quick capture when no existing or new person is ready to be linked. These records should remain `pending_resolution`, appear in review, and stay out of normal person profiles, briefs, drafts, and memory extraction until linked, converted into a new person, archived, or dismissed.

This keeps capture fluid without turning Tendnote into a general unstructured notes app.
