# Surprise Subjects Are Authoritative Gift Plan Exclusions

Phase Eight reuses selected-member Shared Scope for the co-planners of a
member-owned Gift Plan, but a plan whose subject is an active Household Member
may designate that person as its Surprise Subject. The subject is an
authoritative exclusion: Tendnote must prevent the plan, its ideas, provenance,
reminders, counts, source references, summaries, search results, and deep links
from reaching them. This is necessary because an allow-list alone cannot prevent
an accidental later addition or a derived-surface leak, and because an exposed
surprise is irreversibly lost. The rule is deliberately narrow rather than a
generic deny-list permission system: it applies only to the active Household
Member who is the subject of a Gift Plan, while every other record family keeps
its existing private, shared, or household visibility contract. This accepts a
small domain-specific authorization rule in exchange for making surprise
planning genuinely trustworthy without inventing a second general-purpose access
model.
