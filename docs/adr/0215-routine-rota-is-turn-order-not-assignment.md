# Routine Rota Is Turn Order, Not Assignment

Phase Eight gives household-native Routines an optional ordered Routine Rota of active members, and household-native one-time Actions an optional single Turn Holder, superseding the Phase Five deferral of responsibility in ADR 0154 for household-native records only. The rota answers "whose turn is it?" for a couple or small trusted circle while refusing to become task routing: it is explicit and never inferred from completion history, it never gates authority so any member may complete, skip, edit, or pause regardless of turn, advancement moves to the next member in order regardless of who actually acted so covering for a partner is never a claim on their next turn, and Tendnote stores no turn counts, streaks, missed-turn state, fairness score, or balance between members. A named turn produces no push by itself: it may offer that member their own turn-scoped Reminder Schedule once, which they accept or decline, preserving ADR 0203's rule that no member's action enrolls another member's device. Member-owned records carry no rota, and the rota creates no per-member backlog, assignment inbox, or default my-turns filter. This accepts a genuinely new domain concept, and the risk of drifting toward chore-management, in exchange for the one coordination question a shared household actually asks.
