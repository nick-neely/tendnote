# Reminder Opt-In Is Earned And Installation-Scoped

Phase Seven offers Reminder Opt-In only after the owner successfully creates or accepts a notification-eligible time-bound record on that installation, then requires a separate explicit action before requesting platform permission. Consent applies to eligible reminders the owner deliberately creates or accepts on that installation, while inferred suggestions remain silent and Phase Seven avoids per-record push toggles or a notification-preference matrix. Postponement is quiet, denial moves recovery into owner-initiated settings, iOS installation guidance remains separate from permission, and disabling an installation immediately stops its delivery without claiming to revoke browser or operating-system permission. This trades the reach of first-load prompting and granular notification controls for a calmer earned-value moment, predictable installation-scoped consent, and fewer ways to mistake platform permission for successful Tendnote enrollment.
