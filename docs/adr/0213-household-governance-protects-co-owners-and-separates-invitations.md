# Household Governance Protects Co-owners and Separates Invitations

Phase Eight will treat a Household Invitation as a separate, email-address-bound, expiring capability rather than a pending membership, and will enforce the eight-seat policy through one atomic product-policy seam. A Household Workspace has one or more active co-owners: promotion requires the recipient's acceptance, no owner may unilaterally demote or remove another owner, the last owner cannot leave, dissolution requires unanimous active-owner confirmation, and inaccessible-owner recovery is support-only. This deliberately favors explicit, auditable consent over a simpler creator-admin model, while preserving immediate member departure/removal, fresh-invitation re-entry, and the distinction between private/member-owned data and household-native records.
