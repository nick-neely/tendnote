# General Actions Can Enter Scoped Proactive Summaries

Phase 5 due, overdue, and deliberately resurfaced General Actions may appear in scheduled or proactive summaries, including Discord delivery, when the delivery target's scope is allowed to see them. This extends the Phase 4 delivery-scope boundary to Actions and Routines while deferring continuous background scanning, nagging loops, notification-center behavior, external execution, and provider-side task creation.
